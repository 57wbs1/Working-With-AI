Questions

Before you pay.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The course assumes no programming background. The one lesson that builds software does it without you writing any code, and every lab works on a free account.

How long does it actually take?

About twenty hours if you do every lab. Just over two hours of that is reading. It is built as three days but nothing stops you doing it over three weeks, and it remembers where you got to in the same browser.

Will it be out of date in a month?

Parts of it, yes, and the course says so on the page. Prices and model names move constantly, which is why updates are included. The method itself changes far more slowly.

Can I use this for assessed work?

Your institution's rules apply and the course tells you to find them. What it does teach is how to research without inventing citations, which is the thing that actually gets people in trouble.

Is any of it classified or official?

None of it. I built it on a personal account in my own time, using no official material. Lesson 06 and a dedicated lab cover exactly where that line sits and how to stay on the right side of it.

What if it is not for me?

The curriculum is outlined above so you can judge it before you pay. If you still have a question, message 9181 7066 on WhatsApp or Telegram and ask.
